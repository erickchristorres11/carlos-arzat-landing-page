## Users

The visitors are people looking for a financial advisor and insurance agent they can trust. Three confirmed groups:

- **Young professionals**: stable income, want to organize their money, set goals and start saving or investing.
- **People nearing retirement**: worried that their Afore savings won't be enough once inflation is accounted for.
- **Business owners**: variable income and assets to protect.

In every case the visitor's job is the same: decide whether Carlos is credible and approachable enough to message him.

A personal professional landing page for **Carlos Geovanny Arzat Torres**, an insurance agent who specializes in financial advisory and planning. It introduces who he is, his credentials, results, services and working method, and makes it easy to contact him.

## Product Principles

1. **Credibility before persuasion.** Verifiable credentials and real figures go first; no exaggerated claims.
2. **One clear action.** Every section leads to WhatsApp.
3. **Concrete over abstract.** Explain each service with real tools (50/30/20, Presupuesto Power, the Afore projection) instead of generic promises.
4. **A human advisor.** Show the person behind the numbers so the first conversation feels close.
5. **Confidentiality as a value.** Careful handling of the client's information is part of the offer.
